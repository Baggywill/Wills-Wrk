How we work

Four steps from first message to delivery.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Send a message with the item or room, size, and budget. Photos help.

  2. Get a quote and design

    We can visit your site to measure. We agree the design, timber, finish, and price before any cutting starts.

  3. We build it

    Your piece is made in our workshop. We share progress photos.

  4. Delivery and fitting

    We deliver in Kaduna and beyond, install, and fix anything that is not right.

About Will'sWork

Will'sWork is a woodworking and interior design business in Kaduna, built on careful measuring, sound joints, and honest pricing. We design the space, build the furniture for it, and add electronics where they help, so everything fits together.

Whether it is a single table or a whole house, we treat every job the same way: agree the details first, then build it properly.
